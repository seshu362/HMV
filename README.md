# Hindi Mahavidyalaya (HMV) — Official Website Redesign

A creative, premium redesign for **Hindi Mahavidyalaya, Hyderabad** (Autonomous & NAAC Re-Accredited, Affiliated to Osmania University).

Crafted with **React 19**, **Three.js / React Three Fiber**, **GSAP & ScrollTrigger**, **Lenis Smooth Scroll**, and **Tailwind CSS v4**.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

1. **Official Top Header & Announcement Ticker:**
   - Real-time rolling ticker with pause/resume controls for Admissions 2026-27, Convocation notices, and examination results.
   - Institutional helpline (`+91 40 2761 6330`), principal's official email, language switcher (English / हिंदी), accessibility font sizer (`A- A A+`), and accreditation portals (SWAYAM, NAAC, UGC, NIRF, Student Logins).

2. **Sticky Navigation Bar:**
   - Features the official college crest (`logo.svg`).
   - Integrated quick search for notices and programmes.
   - Interactive dropdowns for Admissions and Academics.
   - Responsive mobile navigation drawer with animated burger menu.

3. **Cinematic Hero Photographic Carousel with 3D Interaction:**
   - Full-bleed Unsplash collegiate photography (Grand portico archway entrance, modern library, science labs, and convocation community).
   - Interactive Three.js canvas (`ParticleField.tsx`) with floating wireframe icosahedrons and glowing particle field.
   - Previous/Next navigation controls, timer pause on hover, and smooth slide indicators.
   - Authentic **60th Diamond Jubilee (1966 – 2026)** crest badge in the bottom corner.

4. **Announcements & Examination Circulars (`NoticesSection`):**
   - Filterable board notifications by category: *Admissions, Examinations, Results, Circulars*.
   - Direct PDF download indicators and date stamps.

5. **Legacy & About Section:**
   - Highlights 60+ years of history, Osmania University affiliation, NAAC A+ rating, and autonomous governance.
   - GSAP ScrollTrigger reveals and animated statistics counters.

6. **Academic Programmes Showcase:**
   - Undergraduate & Postgraduate streams: *B.A., B.Com. (General/Computers/Honours), B.Sc. (Maths/Stats/CS/Biotech), B.Voc., BBA, and M.A. / M.Com.*

7. **Campus Life & Infrastructure Parallax Gallery:**
   - Asymmetric masonry grid featuring the heritage main building, central library, high-tech labs, and green campus grounds with scroll-driven parallax effects.

8. **Admissions 2026-27 Stepper & Digital Application Portal:**
   - 4-phase step-by-step guidance from online registration to seat confirmation.
   - Interactive pop-up application form modal with validation and instant submission confirmation.

9. **Comprehensive Institutional Footer:**
   - Academic stream links, governance documents, campus address in Nallakunta, Hyderabad, and quick scroll-to-top button.
